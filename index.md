I am a PhD candidate at the Faculty of Economics and Business, [University of Groningen](https://www.rug.nl/), affiliated with the [OPERA research group](https://www.rug.nl/feb/research/operations/). My research addresses how agentic AI systems can be grounded in explicit domain knowledge so that the support they provide remains reliable in industrial settings where an incorrect instruction has real consequences.

Large language models produce fluent guidance without any commitment to the procedures an organisation actually sanctions. My work treats structured knowledge sources, and graph-based representations in particular, as the mechanism that constrains what an agent is permitted to say and do, and asks under which conditions structured traversal is preferable to unstructured retrieval. The setting is maintenance and operator support, where procedural knowledge is abundant but locked in diagrams and standard operating documents rather than in machine-readable form.

The work runs across three connected lines. The first concerns extracting procedural structure from industrial documentation into graph form. The second concerns grounding and compliance, meaning the conditions under which a generated answer can be traced back to a sanctioned procedure. The third concerns integrating those knowledge sources into agentic systems that support technicians during diagnosis and repair.

My position is funded by AIXPERT, a Horizon Europe project on trustworthy AI for industrial operations, with Philips Drachten as the primary industrial partner. I am supervised by [Christos Emmanouilidis](https://www.rug.nl/staff/c.emmanouilidis/) and [Laura Maruster](https://www.rug.nl/staff/l.maruster/).

Before starting the PhD I completed an MSc in Technology and Operations Management at Groningen, graduating cum laude with a thesis on variational autoencoder based fault detection for wind turbines, validated on NREL benchmark data and on operational data from a Nordex N131 turbine. I hold a BSc in Business Administration from the University of Granada, and I have worked as an AI engineer at Nexler and as an analytics product manager at BridgeU in London.

I am glad to hear from anyone working on knowledge graphs, retrieval grounding, or agentic decision support in industrial operations.
