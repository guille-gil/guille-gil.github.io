## Conference papers

- Gil de Avalle, G., Maruster, L., Raza, S., Emmanouilidis, C. DiagFlowBench: Evaluating how language models handle off-procedure inputs in grounded diagnostic dialogue. *Proceedings of EMNLP 2026: Industry Track*, pp. 1854–1871, 2026. \[[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-industry.108/)\] \[[Code and data](https://github.com/guille-gil/DiagFlowBench)\]

- Reuvers, W. J., Gil de Avalle, G., Maruster, L., Emmanouilidis, C. Procedural correctness in AI-supported maintenance troubleshooting: A comparison of embedding-based and hybrid retrieval. *7th IFAC/INSTICC International Conference on Innovative Intelligent Industrial Production and Logistics (IN4PL 2026)*, Angers, France, 2026. \[[Program entry](https://www.insticc.org/node/TechnicalProgram/in4pl/2026/presentationDetails/153093)\]

- Gil de Avalle, G., Maruster, L., Sloot, E., Emmanouilidis, C. FlowExtract: Procedural knowledge extraction from maintenance flowcharts. *Advances in Production Management Systems: Shaping the Future of Industry Through Sustainable, Data-Driven, and Human-Centric Production Systems (APMS 2026)*, IFIP AICT, Springer, pp. 354–369, 2026. \[[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-38594-9_24)\] \[[Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06770)\] \[[Code](https://github.com/guille-gil/FlowExtract)\]

- Gil de Avalle, G., Maruster, L., Emmanouilidis, C. Procedural knowledge extraction from industrial troubleshooting guides using vision language models. *23rd IFAC World Congress (IFAC 2026)*, 2026. \[[Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22754)\]

- Gil de Avalle Bellido, G., Emmanouilidis, C. Anomaly detection in wind turbines using variational autoencoders and isolation forest. *Advances in Production Management Systems (APMS 2025)*, IFIP AICT 765, Springer, pp. 518–532, 2025. \[[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-03534-9_35)\]
